# ChessSkins Privacy Policy

_Last updated: July 16, 2026_

ChessSkins does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any user data. Period.

- **No analytics or tracking** of any kind.
- **No network requests.** All artwork and code ship inside the extension package;
  nothing is fetched from remote servers.
- **No page data.** The content script only injects a stylesheet on lichess and
  chess.com pages; it does not read page content, game data, or personal information.
- **Local settings only.** Your selected skin and on/off preference are saved with
  `chrome.storage.sync`, which lives in your browser profile (and syncs between your
  own devices if you use Chrome sync). A custom skin you create is saved with
  `chrome.storage.local` on your machine. Uninstalling the extension removes both.

Questions? Open an issue at https://github.com/NikAgs/ChessSkins/issues.
